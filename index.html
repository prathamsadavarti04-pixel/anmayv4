Intelligent Defence.
Securing the Skies.

Anmay Autonomous Systems Private Limited is an Indian technology company developing autonomous defence and airspace-security solutions, with an initial focus on counter-drone systems.

We are building modular technology designed to address evolving low-altitude aerial threats and support future autonomous defence capabilities.

Low-altitude threats are evolving

Small, low-flying and coordinated drones can create new challenges for airspace monitoring and site security. Detecting and tracking these threats reliably—and coordinating an appropriate response—requires systems designed for changing operational conditions.

Anmay Defence is developing counter-drone technology intended to support improved aerial threat awareness and a modular approach to airspace security.

Meet STRIKE

STRIKE stands for Smart Targeting Reconnaissance and Intelligent Kill Engagement.

STRIKE is Anmay Defence’s counter-drone platform, being developed to support aerial threat detection, tracking and airspace-security operations.
